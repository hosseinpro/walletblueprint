Five questions decide
whether a wallet holds.

Hardware wallets share one problem no product fixes: a device whose only purpose is holding Bitcoin marks its owner as worth attacking — distributor databases have been breached, and supply-chain tampering is real even where the vendor ships no backdoors. If you need one anyway, five things stand between an attacker and your seed: the secure element, the trusted screen, the trusted input path, entropy generation, and how much of the stack you can read. No affiliate links, no aggregate star ratings — just the architecture.
